Lesson 03 · Transmission, final drive & differential

How does engine spin reach both front wheels?

Two short interactions trace torque the rest of the way to the road: first through the Direct Shift-CVT, then through the open differential around a corner.

Vehicle locator 02–04 · Driveline
AInteraction 1 · Direct Shift-CVT (Launch → Cruise) Engine input → Launch gear or steel belt → Final drive
Active path: Launch gear (fixed mechanical gear)
Locked (Launch gear)
Torque path Fixed launch gear
Illustrative ratio 3.4 : 1 (fixed)
Output vs input speed 0.29x speed · high torque

Pulling away from a stop needs high torque. In Launch mode, torque flows through meshing steel gears instead of the belt, giving crisp takeoff feel and protecting the belt from extreme low-ratio load.

Simplification note: Ratios shown above are illustrative to demonstrate torque multiplication versus cruising speed. In the real RAV4 Direct Shift-CVT, the transmission control unit decides when to hand off between the launch gear and belt variator using vehicle speed, load and operating conditions, not engine RPM alone.
BInteraction 2 · Open differential & front wheels (Straight → Left → Right) Why do the front wheels turn at different speeds in a corner?
Steering: Straight ahead · Both front wheels turn at the same speed
Spider gear stays locked relative to carrier straight ahead and spins in a turn
Left front wheel 100% of ring speed
Spider gears Stationary on pin
Right front wheel 100% of ring speed

Driving straight ahead, both front tyres cover the same distance. The differential carrier turns both side gears together at the same speed, so the spider gears do not need to spin on their own pin.

Engineering detail · Direct Shift-CVT handoff & open differential speed rule

Why Toyota added a launch gear to a CVT: Conventional belt CVTs lose efficiency when running at very tight pulley radii during standing starts. Offloading low-speed launch duty to a physical gear lets the variator operate in a narrower, higher-efficiency range with smaller pulleys and a wider overall ratio spread (Toyota TNGA Direct Shift-CVT).

Why wheels can spin at different speeds: Inside an open differential, the average angular speed of the two front wheels always equals the ring-gear carrier speed:

(ω_left + ω_right) / 2 = ω_carrier | Example turn: (75% + 125%) / 2 = 100%

Whatever speed the inner wheel gives up around a tighter radius, the spider gears immediately pass to the outer wheel so neither tyre has to scrub across the road.
